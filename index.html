About The Clinic

Dentistry that puts the patient first.

Led by Dr. Zahar Unnissa, every treatment plan is built around clear communication, gentle technique, and long-term oral health, not just the immediate problem.

What We Treat

Dental Fillings

Conservative Dentistry

BPS Dentures
