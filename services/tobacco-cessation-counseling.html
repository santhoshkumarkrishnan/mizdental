Tobacco Cessation Counseling

Supportive, practical guidance to help you reduce or quit tobacco and protect your oral and general health.

Discuss quitting support

Why stopping tobacco matters

Tobacco can increase the risk of gum disease, tooth loss, staining, delayed healing and oral cancer. Stopping is one of the most valuable steps you can take for your mouth and your wider health.

How counseling can help

  • Discuss your current tobacco use and reasons for quitting.
  • Identify triggers and plan practical alternatives.
  • Set a realistic quit or reduction goal.
  • Connect you with medical support when medication or additional care is appropriate.
Is it too late to quit?

No. Stopping tobacco benefits health at every age, even after years of use.

Can a dentist help me quit?

Dental teams can provide motivation, oral health advice and referral support as part of a wider cessation plan.
