Oral Cancer Screening

A preventive examination for changes in the mouth, lips and surrounding tissues that need further attention.

Book an oral screening

Why screening matters

Many oral changes are harmless, but persistent ulcers, patches, lumps or unexplained discomfort should be assessed. Screening supports early professional evaluation and appropriate referral when needed.

What the examination considers

  • Lips, cheeks, tongue, gums, palate and throat area.
  • Persistent sores, colour changes, lumps or restricted movement.
  • Risk factors such as tobacco, alcohol and previous lesions.
  • Clear advice about monitoring or further investigation.
Who should consider oral screening?

Adults, especially those with tobacco or alcohol exposure, persistent mouth changes or other risk factors, may benefit from professional screening.

Does screening diagnose cancer?

Screening identifies changes that need attention; only appropriate specialist tests can confirm a diagnosis.

Do not ignore persistent changes

Early professional assessment is an important part of oral health.
