Services / Cavity Fillings

Tooth-Coloured Cavity Fillings

Repair decay, protect your tooth and restore comfortable function with a carefully planned filling.

Discuss a cavity

When a filling may help

Cavities can grow quietly until they cause sensitivity or pain. A filling removes the damaged portion and seals the tooth to help prevent further decay.

Our approach

  • Examine the tooth and assess the extent of decay.
  • Explain suitable filling options and expected care.
  • Remove decay while preserving healthy tooth structure.
  • Shape and check the restoration for a comfortable bite.
Are tooth-coloured fillings noticeable?

Composite fillings are matched to the tooth shade, making them a discreet choice for many visible teeth.

What happens if a cavity is deep?

A deeper cavity may need additional protection or root canal treatment. Your dentist will explain the options after examination.

Do not ignore tooth pain

Early evaluation can help protect more of your natural tooth.
